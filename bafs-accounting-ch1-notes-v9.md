# 企業會計與財務概論：會計概述複習筆記
# Chapter 1: Overview of Accounting Revision Notes

## 1. 甚麼是企業個體、簿記與會計？
## 1. What are Business Entities, Bookkeeping, and Accounting?

企業個體是指通過售賣貨品或提供服務以賺取利潤的個體。
A business entity refers to an entity that earns profits by selling goods or providing services.

簿記就像是寫日記，它的重點在於記錄日常交易及保存財務記錄。
Bookkeeping is like writing a diary, and its focus is on recording daily transactions and preserving financial records.

會計則是分析日記，它的範疇比簿記更廣，目的是為企業個體提供財務資訊，幫助持份者作出決策。
Accounting is like analyzing a diary, and its scope is broader than bookkeeping, and its purpose is to provide financial information to help stakeholders make decisions.

同學提提你
Student Tip

「簿記」只是手動把發生過的事寫下來；「會計」則是把這些記錄整理、分析，變成一份老闆和投資者都能看懂的「成績單」。
"Bookkeeping" is just writing down what happened by hand; "Accounting" is organizing and analyzing these records into a "report card" that owners and investors can understand.

一般而言，會計資訊會以財務報表的形式展示。
Generally, accounting information is presented in the form of financial statements.

### (a) 會計的目的
### (a) Purposes of Accounting

反映企業的財務表現，不論是錄得利潤或是虧損。
To reflect the financial performance of a business, whether making a profit or incurring a loss.

報告企業的財務狀況，包括資產、負債及資本。
To report the financial position of a business, including assets, liabilities and capital.

為東主提供有關企業優勢和劣勢的資訊，以便作出進一步的投資決策。
To provide information for owners about the strengths and weaknesses of a business for further investment decisions.

### (b) 會計的角色
### (b) Role of Accounting

有系統地記錄財務交易，並編製成會計資訊，以供管理人員作出商業決策。
To systematically record financial transactions and compile them into accounting information for management to make business decisions.

## 2. 會計的四種主要功能
## 2. Four Main Functions of Accounting

會計程序由以下四個核心步驟組成。
The accounting process consists of the following four core steps.

第一步：記錄 ──> 第二步：分類 ──> 第三步：歸納 ──> 第四步：溝通
Step 1: Recording ──> Step 2: Classifying ──> Step 3: Summarising ──> Step 4: Communicating

要記住會計的功能，老師教你一個口訣，取其英文首字母。
To remember the functions of accounting, the teacher has a formula for you: "R-C-S-C" (taking the first letter of each English word).

### (a) 記錄交易事項
### (a) Recording Transactions

這是會計的基本功能，也是會計程序的首個步驟。
This is the primary function of accounting and the initial step in the accounting process.

企業須保存財務交易記錄，作為編製會計資訊的基礎。
A business needs to keep records of its financial transactions as the basis for preparing accounting data.

考試關鍵字：帳簿、電子檔案、原始數據。
Exam Keywords: books, electronic files, raw data.

課文實例：
Textbook Example:

班會財政碧琪負責記錄影印和購買文具等各項交易，並妥善保存支出的收據。
Peggy, the class financial secretary, recorded transactions like photocopying and purchasing stationery, and kept all receipts properly.

日常應用：
Daily Application:

零售業常用銷售點系統。
Retail businesses commonly use a point-of-sale system.

收銀員使用條碼掃瞄器掃瞄條碼後，交易資訊會自動記錄在電腦內。
After the cashier scans the barcode with a barcode reader, the transaction information is automatically recorded in the computer.

### (b) 分類已記錄的數據
### (b) Classifying Recorded Data

指按交易事項的性質，有系統地將記錄下來的數據歸類。
Refers to the systematic grouping of recorded data according to the nature of each transaction.

這樣做的目的是為了方便管理人員了解企業的表現。
This is done to facilitate management's understanding of the business performance.

考試關鍵字：資產、負債、資本、收益、費用。
Exam Keywords: assets, liabilities, capital, revenues, expenses.

課文實例：
Textbook Example:

四甲班班會將購買圓珠筆、鉛筆及直尺等支出，統一歸納為「文具費」。
Peggy groups purchases of pens, pencils and rulers under "stationery expenses".

超級市場將向顧客銷售水果、蔬菜、肉類及飲料等收入，全部歸入「銷貨」或「營業額」。
A supermarket groups sales revenues of fruits, vegetables, meat and drinks under "sales" or "turnover".

### (c) 歸納已分類的資訊
### (c) Summarising Classified Information

指將已分類的數據，以容易理解和具意義的方式展示出來。
Involves the presentation of classified data in an understandable and meaningful way.

一般而言，企業會以財務報表的形式展示這些歸納後的數據。
Generally, businesses present these summarised data in the form of financial statements.

考試關鍵字：財務報表，包括損益表和財務狀況表。
Exam Keywords: financial statements (Statement of Profit or Loss, Statement of Financial Position).

課文實例：
Textbook Example:

碧琪在每月月終總結該月的總收入、總支出和餘額，展示資金運用情況。
Peggy summarised the total of receipts, spending and remaining funds at the end of each month.

### (d) 溝通
### (d) Communicating

將已歸納的會計資訊編製成財務報表向使用者溝通。
Communicating summarised accounting information to users through financial statements.

這是會計的最後一個步驟，也是最重要的功能。
This is the last step and the most important function of accounting.

這能協助使用者更了解企業的財務表現，從而作出相關的商業決定。
This helps users better understand the financial performance of a business to make appropriate business decisions.

考試關鍵字：解釋、比率、圖表、簡單用語。
Exam Keywords: explanation, ratios, charts, simple terms.

課文實例：
Textbook Example:

碧琪編製收支報表與班會同學進行溝通，主席參考報表後，計劃向同學徵收班會費。
Peggy presents the monthly statement to the classmates, and the chairperson uses this information to plan for raising money.

溫馨提示
Small Tip

溝通功能是為了讓外行人也能明白會計資訊。
The communicating function is to make outsiders understand accounting information as well.

例如：會計師會將專業術語「非流動資產」轉化為「企業擁有的長期資源」，這就是溝通功能的體現。
For example, an accountant converting the technical term "non-current assets" into "long-term resources owned by the business" is an expression of the communication function.

## 3. 常見的兩種財務報表
## 3. Two Popular Forms of Financial Statements

### (a) 損益表
### (a) Income Statement

損益表又稱為全面收益表。
Income statement is also known as the statement of comprehensive income.

它總結公司在特定期間內有關利潤或虧損的財務表現。
It summarises the financial performance (its profit and loss) of a company over a period of time.

### (b) 財務狀況表
### (b) Statement of Financial Position

財務狀況表以往稱為資產負債表。
Statement of financial position was formerly known as the balance sheet.

它總結公司在特定日期的財務狀況，即資產、負債和資本。
It summarises the financial position (i.e., the assets, liabilities and capital) of a company on a given date.

## 4. 財務會計與管理會計
## 4. Financial Accounting vs Management Accounting

會計主要分為兩個範疇。
Accounting is mainly divided into two branches.

財務會計就像是「給家長看的成績單」，管理會計則是「給玩家自己看的作戰秘笈」。
Financial accounting is like a "report card for parents", while management accounting is like a "battle guide for players".

### (a) 財務會計（成績單）
### (a) Financial Accounting (Report Card)

主要功能是向內部使用者和外部使用者報告企業的財務資訊。
The primary function is to report the financial information of a business to both internal and external users.

內部使用者包括東主和管理人員。
Internal users include owners and managers.

外部使用者包括潛在投資者、債權人和稅務局。
External users include potential investors, creditors and the Inland Revenue Department.

財務會計記錄歷史數據，並以一系列符合既定標準的財務報表展示。
Financial accounting records historical data and presents them in standardised financial statements.

這些報表會定期編製，一般是每月、每季、每半年或每年一次。
These statements are prepared regularly, usually monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or annually.

### (b) 一般有借方餘額的帳戶出現了貸方餘額的處理
### (b) Treatment of Debit Balance Accounts Resulting in Credit Balances

企業的銀行存款帳戶可以有貸方餘額或借方餘額。
A business's bank account can have either a credit balance or a debit balance.

假如銀行存款帳戶有貸方餘額，這表示企業虧欠銀行款項，即銀行透支。
If a bank account has a credit balance, it means the business owes money to the bank, which is a bank overdraft.

銀行透支被視為企業的負債，在試算表中反映為貸方餘額。
A bank overdraft is treated as a liability and reflected as a credit balance in the trial balance.

一些預期有借方餘額的帳戶在期末卻出現貸方餘額，這可能是錯誤入帳或遺漏帳目所致。
Some accounts expected to have debit balances resulting in credit balances at period end may be caused by incorrect entries or omissions.

### (c) 管理會計（作戰秘笈）
### (c) Management Accounting (Battle Guide)

為企業內部參與日常營運的管理人員提供資訊，協助他們作出商業決定。
Provides information to internal managers involved in daily operations to help them make business decisions.

這些資訊一般具前瞻性，與以記錄歷史數據為主的財務會計不同。
This information is generally forward-looking, unlike financial accounting which records historical data.

管理會計的展示方式完全視乎管理人員的需要，不受法例和會計準則限制。
The format depends entirely on the needs of management, without regulations by laws and accounting standards.

它沒有固定的編製時距。
It has no regular interval specified for preparation.

### (d) 成本會計與會計準則
### (d) Cost Accounting and Accounting Standards

#### 成本會計：
#### Cost Accounting:

是管理會計的分支。
It is a branch of management accounting.

主要涉及分析生產過程或提供服務期間的各種成本。
It is concerned with analysing different types of cost involved in production or service provision.

它協助管理人員計劃、控制及改善流程以提高效率。
It assists managers to plan, control and improve processes to enhance efficiency.

#### 會計準則：
#### Accounting Standards:

為會計實務制訂原則和標準，讓不同企業的財務報表能以劃一的標準格式展示。
Sets principles and standards for accounting practice so that financial statements can be presented in a standard way.

這便於使用者比較不同企業的表現。
This allows users to compare the performances of different businesses.

## 5. 會計在商業決策上的角色
## 5. Roles of Accounting in Decision-making

企業在作出商業決策時，會計主要在計劃和控制兩個方面發揮作用。
Accounting plays a vital role in business decision-making through planning and controlling.

### (a) 計劃
### (a) Planning

協助管理人員制定目標，並計劃如何達到目標。
Assists management in setting goals and making plans to achieve them.

## 6. 會計師的角色
## 6. Roles of Accountants

在企業中，會計師負責編製和報告會計資訊，主要職責如下。
Accountants are responsible for compiling and reporting accounting information, with major roles as follows.

### (a) 指示簿記員編製會計記錄
### (a) To Guide Bookkeepers to Prepare Accounting Records

確保簿記程序正確完成，並為簿記員提供記錄會計資訊的指引。
Ensures bookkeeping is done correctly and provides guidance on how to record accounting data.

### (b) 匯報財務資訊
### (b) To Report Financial Information

運用已記錄的資訊編製財務報表，總結企業的財務狀況和表現。
Uses recorded data to prepare financial statements summarising financial position and performance.

### (c) 提醒管理人員須注意的業務問題
### (c) To Draw Managers' Attention to Problems

協助不具備足夠會計知識的管理人員正確詮釋財務數據。
Helps managers without sufficient accounting knowledge interpret financial data properly.

例如：及時發現並提醒現金不足以償還貸款的問題，建議籌集資金以避免企業不能還款。
For example: drawing attention to cash shortages for loan repayment and suggesting raising funds.

### (d) 向管理人員提供意見
### (d) To Make Suggestions to Managers

主動參與改善企業營運。
Actively participates in improving business operations.

例如：發現某項開支增幅過高時，告知管理人員並建議節省成本的控制措施。
For example: advising managers on cost-saving control measures when specific expenses rise too high.

課文案例：
Textbook Example:

會計師發現傳統耳機銷貨收入下跌了百分之五十，而藍芽耳機銷貨收入已佔總耳機收入的百分之七十五以上。
The accountant reports traditional earphone revenue dropped by 50%, while Bluetooth earphone revenue contributes over 75%.

行政總裁參考此建議後，決定投放更多資源推廣藍芽耳機，並停止宣傳及售賣舊款傳統耳機。
The CEO considers this information to focus resources on promoting Bluetooth earphones and stop selling traditional ones.

## 7. 會計循環的流程
## 7. Flow of the Accounting Cycle

會計循環是指企業在一個會計期（通常為期一年）內，記錄和處理會計數據的標準程序。
The accounting cycle refers to the standard procedure for recording and processing accounting data within an accounting period (usually one year).

企業須在每個會計期重複整個流程的每個步驟。
A business must repeat each step of the entire process in every accounting period.

### 步驟 1：把交易記錄在原始分錄簿
### Step 1: Record transactions in books of original entry

企業會保存和核對原始單據（例如發票和收據）的交易詳情，然後把交易記錄在相關的原始分錄簿（又稱日記簿）中。
A business collects and verifies transaction details with source documents (such as invoices and receipts) first, and then enters the transactions into related books of original entry (also known as day books).

### 步驟 2：把原始分錄簿上的記錄過帳到分類帳
### Step 2: Post transactions to ledgers

根據複式記帳法，把原始分錄簿記錄的交易事項過帳到分類帳帳戶中。
Post transactions recorded in books of original entry to ledger accounts applying the double-entry bookkeeping system.

### 步驟 3：結平帳戶並編製試算表
### Step 3: Balance off accounts and prepare a trial balance

計算各帳戶餘額，定期進行帳戶結平並在試算表上列出餘額，以檢查複式記帳運算是否準確。
Calculate ledger balances to balance off accounts and list them in a trial balance to check the arithmetical accuracy of double-entry bookkeeping.

考場防錯警示
Exam Trap Alert

試算表平衡不代表帳目百分之百正確。
Trial balance agreement does not mean the accounts are 100% correct.

它只能檢查「算術準確度」，無法發現「原則性錯誤」或「完全遺漏記錄」的錯誤。
It can only check "arithmetical accuracy", but cannot detect errors of principle or complete omission of transactions.

期末調整必須在編製財務報表之前完成。
Period-end adjustments must be completed before the preparation of financial statements.

### 步驟 4：進行期末調整
### Step 4: Make period-end adjustments

在報告期末按應計項目、預付項目及資產價值變動進行期末調整，以反映實際的財務狀況和表現。
At the end of a period, make adjustments for accruals, prepayments and changes in asset values to reflect the true financial position and performance.

### 步驟 5：在會計期末編製財務報表
### Step 5: Prepare financial statements at the end of the accounting period

會計期間完結時，利用調整後的資料編製財務報表（通常包括損益表和財務狀況表），用以匯報企業的財務表現和狀況。
At the end of a period, use adjusted data to prepare financial statements (usually including the income statement and the statement of financial position) to report the financial performance and position.
